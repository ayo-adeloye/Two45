# One official pick stream with routine independent-model review

The scheduled independent raw model now prioritizes fixtures carrying a current legacy PICK and records an exact-market comparison alongside its immutable forecast. Routine refreshes can continue until five minutes before kickoff; prospective evaluation still freezes the last snapshot at least one hour before kickoff and excludes later updates. It still generates probabilities without bookmaker inputs. Comparisons and research forecasts do not create a second ticket or enter the official Record.

Fresh candidates for High Conviction, Elite 3 and Weekend Ladder pass through the same review policy before publication/combination. The existing model must independently qualify the candidate first; the review cannot manufacture eligibility, raise a probability/confidence score, lower quality thresholds, or introduce a market the legacy process did not qualify. High Conviction returns one primary per fixture; Elite and Ladder retain their fixture/rung exclusion rules.

The comparison uses the exact fixture, kickoff, market and normalized selection/line at the current verified Bet365 price. Source forecast/model IDs and both probabilities travel with the final selection into its publication snapshot. Holds append research-only receipts to `two45_selection_shadow_v2` under the separate review version; they never enter the Record. Repeated identical holds are suppressed within a worker, and any repeats across workers remain research receipts, not additional picks or evaluation observations. Independent support is labelled SUPPORTS, smaller/value-positive differences are DIFFERS, and missing/stale/weak/unsupported evidence is explicitly UNREVIEWED. Missing evidence is not agreement.

A HOLD requires adequate independent evidence (quality >=0.75, sample >=8, league effective sample >=20, no quality hold), an immutable dataset hash, pre-match timestamps, a forecast <=60 minutes old, current feature/training boundaries and a quote/provider update <=30 minutes old. The raw independent estimate must be at least 10 percentage points below the legacy probability and imply EV <=-5% at that exact price. These are conservative review thresholds, not fitted or validated confidence bounds. A held market is removed from fresh official combinations; an alternative still needs to pass every original qualification rule. Subsequent scheduled price/data/model passes can reconsider it. Existing locked tickets, publication snapshots and settlement receipts are unchanged.

The V2 replacement and raw v3.1 model have NOT been promoted. Proper-score/ROI validation blockers and launch gates remain. Official selection is described as LEGACY_WITH_INDEPENDENT_REVIEW, not a validated ensemble. Agreement contributes zero confidence boost. Review influence must be measured prospectively before any stronger weighting, averaging, automatic model-originated picks or full replacement.

No extra football-provider calls or synchronous raw-model fitting are added to the legacy analysis path. Official review reads are projected, batched and cached for 30 seconds; the independent model keeps its bounded scheduled inference. Its raw prospective selection cohort is unchanged. The review policy has its own version, `two45-independent-review-v1`; its prospective impact can be reconstructed from immutable source predictions/comparisons and official publication snapshots, separately from raw-model validation.

Tests: `node model-review-v1.test.mjs` and `node tests/model-review-runtime.cjs`, plus the existing core, publication, pricing, overnight, Record and Ladder regressions. Source/bundle checks are part of deployment. Roll back through a new code revert while preserving research, locked tickets and the publication ledger.
