# Two45 Independent Model v1.7

Deployed September 28, 2026.

Changes:
- Sportsbook odds are now confirmation/value input, not a hard qualification gate.
- High-confidence independent model selections can qualify through MODEL_CONVICTION even without mapped odds.
- Non-1X2 markets receive a preference in ranking (goals, double chance, team goals, BTTS).
- Weak/low-quality data still produces NO_BET.
- Expanded priority coverage for major international competitions, qualifiers, Libertadores/Sudamericana, and additional European leagues.
- Priority international/major fixtures move through the analysis queue sooner, including tomorrow fixtures after 8 PM Eastern.
- Model version: two45-independent-v1.7.
